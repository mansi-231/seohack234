Quick Reference

The 5-Minute Checklist for Every New Page

Before you hit publish on any article or blog post, ensure you have ticked these beginner fundamentals:

  • ✓ Unique Title Tag: Includes your primary topic clearly under 60 characters.
  • ✓ Meta Description: A honest, compelling 1-2 sentence summary under 155 characters.
  • ✓ Single H1 Heading: Only one clear H1 at the top of the page.
  • ✓ Descriptive Image Alt Text: Explains what the image depicts for visually impaired users and bots.
  • ✓ Internal Links: Links to at least 2 relevant related articles on your own site.
